# nbody_streams logo spec

The wordmark has four parts:

- "nbody" in Manrope Light. Its o carries two isophote contours.
- A stream with a progenitor that rises into a calligraphic t and r.
- "eam" in Manrope SemiBold.
- A final s drawn as a two-arm spiral galaxy, with a glowing core and star clusters.

Every asset is generated by `source/build_logo.py`. Edit that script, or its data file, rather than the SVGs.

```bash
python plots/logo/source/build_logo.py
```

The script needs numpy and Pillow. It also needs the system librsvg and cairo (`librsvg-2.so.2`, `libcairo.so.2`), which it loads through ctypes to render the PNGs.

The README switches between the light and dark logo with `<picture>`:

```html
<picture>
  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="plots/logo/nbody_streams_logo_dark.svg">
  <img src="plots/logo/nbody_streams_logo_light.svg" width="480" alt="nbody_streams">
</picture>
```

GitHub wraps this in its own `<themed-picture>` element, which follows the GitHub appearance setting. The palette is also chosen so that either variant stays readable on either background, so a mismatched theme still shows a usable logo.

## Coordinates

The SVGs use font units, with an x-height of X = 540 and the baseline at y = 758.4. The y-axis points down. The ink box runs from (270.1, 38) to the galaxy's right edge, and down to y = 1002.6 at the bottom of the y descender. The viewBox pads the ink by 4 % on every side. On the right, the padding is measured from where the galaxy's disk glow fades out (0.8 of its radius).

## Construction

**"nbody", "eam".** These are the Manrope outlines from v2.4.0, stored in `logo_source.json` under `glyphs`. The SemiBold e, a and m are shifted right so that the e starts 14 units left of the r's flag tip.

**o isophotes.** There are two rings of uniform width, centred on contours traced from the trial artwork:

- An outer boxy ring, 24 units wide, Fourier-smoothed to 7 harmonics.
- An inner ellipse, 19 units wide, smoothed to 3 harmonics.

The o outline itself is unchanged from v2.4.0.

**Stream, t and r.** These were traced from the trial artwork and include:

- the ribbon stream, with a crescent trailing arm
- the calligraphic t, with a wedge top and curled foot
- the r, whose head grows out of the crossbar

The traced outline was lightly smoothed, with corners kept sharp. The seam where the trial's ribbon folds into the r head was replaced by a single cubic curve. The stored polygons are `str_main` (leading arm + t + r) and `str_tail` (trailing arm). The renderer draws each polygon as cubic Béziers that pass through its points.

The stream fill is a horizontal gradient: muted at the trailing tip, mid-gradient at 55 %, and text colour at x = 3990, which is reached by the t. The progenitor is a circle at (3454.2, 512.6) with r = 79.0. It has a text-colour ring and a pale fill of radius 0.76 r.

**Galaxy s.** A barred two-arm logarithmic spiral. Parameters in units of the scale H:

- Spiral: bar position angle 170°, 160° of winding per arm, half-bar 0.07, radius 0.5 at the tip, r(t) = 0.07 exp[ln(0.5/0.07) t^0.85].
- Projection: x compressed by 0.64 (inclination).
- Width: full width 0.185 H, tapering as (1 − u^1.6)^0.7, and pinched to 0.7 at the bar.
- Size: H is set so the galaxy's ink height is 575 units, the same as the e and a. The ink bottom sits at y = 776.4, and the left ink edge 50 units right of the m.
- Arm fill: a gradient along the tip-to-tip axis, going tip colour → text (0.3) → text (0.7) → tip colour.
- Layers, from back to front:
  1. A disk glow: an ellipse of 0.50 × 0.42 H rotated −25°, filled with a radial gradient whose opacity goes 1 → 0.4 → 0.
  2. The arms.
  3. A core halo: an ellipse 2.1 times the core size, with radial opacity 0.75 (light) or 0.85 (dark) → 0.
  4. A crisp core: an ellipse of 0.115 × 0.095 H aligned with the projected bar. It has no outline.
  5. Star clusters, placed by a fixed random seed (11):
     - 5 knots per arm on the arm (r = 0.009–0.016 H)
     - 5 stars per arm just outside it (r = 0.005–0.010 H)
     - 7 stars around the core (r = 0.005–0.009 H)

**Mark.** The galaxy alone, centred in a 512 x 512 square, with ink height 0.62 of the square and the disk glow scaled by 0.92. It still reads as a spiral at 32 px.

**Social preview.** A white 1280 x 640 card. The light wordmark's ink is 980 px wide and centred. The tagline sits 46 px below the ink, and the logo and tagline are centred together vertically.

## History

v2.4.0 (2026-11-22) used Claude Design geometry with near-black `#2e2424` and cream `#f6ede8` text. The current redesign (2026-11-24) made these changes:

- Took the stream, t, r and o isophotes from a hand-made trial redraw.
- Replaced the galaxy.
- Switched to the theme-proof palette above, because the cream dark variant was unreadable when it landed on a white page.
